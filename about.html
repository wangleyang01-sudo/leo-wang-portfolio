A little about me

Hi, I'm
Leo.

I'm a London-based photographer and curator interested in the stories held in a look, a place, and the details we might otherwise pass by.

My work moves between fashion, portraiture, culture, and commissioned projects. I like images that feel considered but never overworked—made with a sense of openness, a little patience, and a close attention to people.

When I'm not behind the camera, I work on independent curatorial projects that create space for new voices and different ways of seeing. I work across the UK and internationally.
